EXPERIMENT - lexicon-category features + negation vs TF-IDF-only baseline
========================================================================

Ground truth: News\Build_sentiment_label\Traditional_ML\Common\tune_holdout\ground_truth_holdout.csv
  314 rows (neg 92 / neu 155 / pos 67)
Repeated stratified 5-fold CV, 10 repeats, 2000 bootstrap resamples. TF-IDF fit per fold (leak-free); lexicon features are fixed word lists (Seed_set_Prepare + Loughran & McDonald 2011 negation rule), not fit from data, so adding them leaks nothing.
Feature columns added: negative_prop, positive_prop, uncertainty_prop, litigious_prop, strong_modal_prop, weak_modal_prop, constraining_prop, net_polarity, coverage
NB family (Multinomial/Complement) skipped: net_polarity can be negative.
